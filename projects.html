01
Web Application

EngiCalc

EngiCalc is a web-based engineering student platform designed to make technical calculations and formula reference faster and more convenient. It features a scientific calculator with essential engineering functions, calculation history, memory operations, and an organized engineering formula library.

The platform also includes formula search and an admin panel for managing engineering formulas, making it a practical digital tool for students to access and organize important engineering resources in one place.

EarthPulse

EarthPulse is a web-based environmental monitoring platform designed to provide real-time insights into weather, air quality, and global environmental conditions.

The platform combines interactive maps, location-based weather information, environmental data, and live updates into a single user-friendly dashboard.

EngiCalc 2.0

EngiCalc 2.0 is a comprehensive web-based engineering calculator platform designed to simplify technical calculations for engineering students.

It includes a Scientific Calculator along with dedicated tools for Matrix, Vector, Complex Numbers, Ohm’s Law, Kirchhoff’s Laws, Projectile Motion, Unit Conversion, and Statistics.
